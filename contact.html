A LITTLE HELP

Good to know.

How do I make an enquiry?

Add products from the collection, review quantities, enter your name and mobile number, then prepare your WhatsApp enquiry. Review the message before sending.

Are the displayed prices final?

Prices are catalogue estimates. Our team confirms current rates, stock and delivery details. Items marked “Rate pending” are excluded from the estimated total.

Can I save my product list?

Yes. On the enquiry page, choose “Print / save enquiry as PDF”, then select Save as PDF in your browser’s print dialog.

Can I pay on this website?

This website collects product enquiries. Contact our team to confirm the next steps; online checkout is not enabled.
